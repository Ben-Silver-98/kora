Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Who we are

KORA is a meal prep container brand operated by KORA Ltd. References to "we", "us", or "KORA" in this policy mean the company operating this website.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: hello@koracontainers.com

What data we collect and why

Waitlist sign-ups

When you join our waitlist we collect your email address. We use this solely to notify you when products become available for purchase. We will not send you marketing emails beyond this unless you separately opt in.

Pre-order enquiries

When you submit a pre-order we collect your name, email address, and product/quantity preference. We use this to contact you to confirm your order and arrange payment. This data is not shared with third parties.

Contact form

When you contact us via the form we collect your name, email address, and message. We use this only to respond to your enquiry.

Legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

For waitlist sign-ups: consent — you provide your email address voluntarily.
For pre-orders and contact enquiries: legitimate interest — to fulfil your request and respond to your message.

How long we keep your data

Waitlist emails are held until the product launches, at which point we will either fulfil your request or delete your data within 30 days. Pre-order and contact data is retained for up to 12 months after your enquiry is resolved.

Who we share your data with

Form submissions are processed via Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form service. They act as a data processor on our behalf and do not use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell or share your personal data with any other third party.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@koracontainers.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website does not currently use cookies or tracking scripts.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
