We built what we couldn't find.

KORA started from a simple frustration — and a drawer full of containers that didn't deserve to be there.

The idea behind the system.

We were tracking macros, hitting the gym five times a week, and still losing time every Sunday to a chaotic kitchen drawer. Containers that didn't stack. Lids that didn't match. Plastic that stained after the first chicken and rice. It was embarrassing.

We looked for a better solution. There wasn't one. Not for the price. Not for the person who actually cares about precision — not just aesthetics. Everything on the market was either cheap and flimsy, or expensive and over-designed for a lifestyle shoot, not a real kitchen.

So we built KORA. Designed from the first principle: remove the friction between your plan and your plate. Every decision — the lid mechanism, the volume markings, the way containers nest — was made with a real meal prep week in mind.

What KORA stands for.

Precision

Meal prep is a numbers game. Volume markings aren't optional — they're the point. Every container is sized and marked so you never have to guess.

Durability

BPA-free materials that don't stain, don't scratch, and don't hold the smell of last Tuesday's salmon. Built to outlast the shelf lives of the food you put in them.

Simplicity

One lid. Works on every container. Locks in four points. We cut the feature list down until only what mattered remained. Then we built that.

Craft

The tolerance between containers. The click of the lid. The way seven containers line up in a fridge. Details that don't get mentioned, but that you notice when they're wrong.

A note from the founder

"I got tired of the drawer. The mismatched lids, the containers that warped after two weeks, the ones that leaked through my bag on the tube. I wanted something I was actually proud to pull out at the gym. KORA is that thing. We built it for people who don't cut corners anywhere else — and shouldn't have to cut them here either."

— Alex, Founder of KORA
